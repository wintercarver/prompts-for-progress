# Lossfunk preserves one completed and three failed research attempts

The four ideas share one research workflow. A multi-agent reinforcement-learning idea failed during implementation, two world-model ideas failed during evaluation, and a semantic-entropy jailbreak-detection paper completed the pipeline.

The set is a compact example of why campaigns should remain connected. Publishing only the completed paper would hide three quarters of the attempted research and erase the reported failure modes.
