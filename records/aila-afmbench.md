# AILA benchmarks agents on physical microscopy tasks

AFMBench contains 100 expert-designed tasks, each evaluated in three independent trials. GPT-4o had a reported 29% error rate across the 300 task instances, while the other tested models had error rates between 51.6% and 66.6%.

The paper also reports successful real-instrument demonstrations. Keeping those beside the failures prevents a polished demonstration from obscuring the reliability and safety evidence.
