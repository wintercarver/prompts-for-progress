# A-Lab GPSS runs 352 spinel-electrolyte experiments

Humans designed the first 77 samples and 12 later samples. The agent proposed and executed 263 samples while using abnormality detection, inductive pattern finding, Bayesian optimization, and a reflection memory.

The public experiment table is more informative than a success label: it covers 19 metals and 124 of 171 possible pairwise metal combinations, and it retains every unsuccessful sample alongside the improving hit rate.
