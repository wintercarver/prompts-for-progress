# AstroAgents generates hypotheses from meteorite spectra

The system divides work among a data analyst, planner, three domain scientists, accumulator, literature reviewer, and critic. The public site makes the role prompts visible and allows the Claude and Gemini outputs to be compared.

This record remains partial because the outcome is a set of expert-rated research proposals. No new astrobiological mechanism was established by experiment.
