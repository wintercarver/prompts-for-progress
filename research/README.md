# Research workspace

This directory preserves the raw evidence and editorial reasoning used to construct the archive.

The initial search is **bounded near-exhaustive**, not absolutely exhaustive. Every research release should state its cutoff date, searched sources, query families, citation-chasing procedure, and known blind spots.

Candidates should remain in the register even when excluded, duplicated, or found to be unsupported. This makes the search auditable and prevents repeated rediscovery.

## Research tracks

- [Cross-prompt methods syntheses](syntheses/README.md) — general, problem-agnostic reviews of prompting methods and evidence.
- [Problem selection and prompt-design studies](problem-studies/README.md) — targeted selection/design campaigns that may cite a synthesis without recreating one.
- [Compound research checkpoints](projects/README.md) — intentional combinations of both tracks.
