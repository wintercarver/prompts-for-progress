# Candidate capture

Capture candidates before imposing a final record schema. At minimum, note:

- working title;
- discovery source and discovery date;
- strongest primary sources;
- people and organizations involved;
- research question and claimed contribution;
- AI system's reported role;
- outcome as reported;
- prompt, transcript, code, paper, and verification availability;
- important dates;
- caveats, disputes, duplication, and follow-up work;
- triage status.
