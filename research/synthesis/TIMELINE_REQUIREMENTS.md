# Timeline and summary-statistics requirements

The site should allow visitors to see the changing rate of documented AI-assisted research activity without collapsing distinct events into one misleading date.

## Candidate date dimensions

- problem or conjecture origin date;
- attempt/run start and end;
- first public disclosure;
- preprint or paper publication;
- formal or computational verification;
- independent expert assessment;
- peer-review acceptance or publication;
- correction, dispute, retraction, or supersession.

Unknown, approximate, and inferred dates must be distinguishable from exact dates.

## Candidate summary dimensions

- attempts and reported outcomes over time;
- complete, partial, disputed, rediscovered, and unsuccessful outcomes;
- field and subfield;
- model and organization;
- AI contribution role;
- prompt and transcript availability;
- validation modes and current status;
- human interaction pattern;
- duration and cost when reported.

Counts must be described as **documented cases in the archive**, not estimates of all AI-assisted research. Publication and discovery bias should remain visible.
