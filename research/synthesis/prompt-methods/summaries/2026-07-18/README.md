# Prompt-methods synthesis — 18 July 2026

This is the initial dated, cross-domain methods snapshot. Its canonical artifacts are the [narrative summary](summary.md) and [case coding matrix](case-matrix.csv).

## Scope and boundary

- Analytic unit: prompt manifest/case, with workflow stage used where helpful; generated corpus rows and logs were not counted as independent prompts.
- Coverage: 23 local prompt manifests and their linked case, campaign, or attempt records, as described in the summary.
- Original checkpoint: the analysis was first recorded in commit `845a40d` on 18 July 2026.
- Rights: the analysis incorporates 11 manifests under `private-rights-review`. It is an **internal methodology snapshot**, not material cleared for public release or broad derivative use.
- Claims: descriptive and mechanism-based only. The heterogeneous archive cannot establish causal prompt effectiveness.

The report was first assembled in the 18 July 2026 pilot checkpoint and was refactored into this recurring-series structure without retaining its separate problem-selection exercise or task-specific research artifacts. It can inform later work, but the playbook and the archive's approved corpus should govern future public-facing syntheses.
