# Initial search protocol

## Objective

Find publicly documented attempts, available by the research cutoff, to use AI systems to make original progress on research-level mathematical and scientific questions. Give priority to cases with preserved prompts, transcripts, code, formalizations, papers, or independent evaluation.

## Search layers

1. Existing surveys, review papers, community lists, and benchmark retrospectives.
2. Primary lab, researcher, repository, paper, preprint, and formalization sources cited by those collections.
3. Scholarly search using combinations of AI-system terms, research-progress terms, outcome terms, and discipline names.
4. Repository and web-community search for prompt or transcript artifacts that papers omit.
5. Backward and forward citation chasing from every included case.
6. Named-person and named-problem searches for corrections, criticism, independent checking, and subsequent use.

## Evidence priorities

Prefer, in order:

1. prompt, transcript, code, formal proof, experimental data, or other direct artifact;
2. resulting paper or preprint;
3. first-person account by the responsible researcher;
4. independent expert evaluation;
5. institutional announcement;
6. secondary reporting or social posts used only for discovery.

## Search logging

For each search session record:

- date and researcher;
- database, site, or source collection;
- exact query or navigation path;
- results reviewed and candidates added;
- citations followed;
- access limitations and likely blind spots.

## Initial boundaries

- Language: English-language discovery first; record this as a known bias.
- Fields: mathematics and mathematically or computationally intensive sciences first.
- Public evidence: private or merely rumored cases are excluded.
- Contribution: routine summarization, writing assistance, and ordinary coding are excluded unless tied to a claimed original research contribution.
- Cutoff: recorded separately for each research release.
