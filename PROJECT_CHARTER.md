# Project charter

## Purpose

Prompts for Progress aims to build an empirical record of how AI-assisted mathematical and scientific research succeeds, fails, and is validated.

The archive is intended to:

1. document attempts to make original research progress with AI systems;
2. preserve the prompts, workflows, and surrounding context when possible;
3. make patterns across attempts legible to researchers and AI agents;
4. encourage responsible experimentation and better research-agent methods.

## Initial inclusion criterion

A case is in scope when it documents an intentional use of an AI system to make original progress on a research-level mathematical or scientific question.

Outcomes may include:

- a complete result;
- a partial result, improved bound, new construction, or counterexample;
- a disputed or later-corrected claim;
- rediscovery of an existing result;
- an unsuccessful documented attempt.

An unsuccessful run is called a **documented attempt**, not a null result, unless it was conducted under a sufficiently defined experimental protocol to justify stronger language.

## Editorial principles

- **Provenance over publicity.** Prefer primary artifacts and preserve the chain from prompt to claim to validation.
- **Attribution by role.** Credit problem selection, prompt authorship, system operation, mathematical or scientific contribution, verification, formalization, and exposition separately when the evidence permits.
- **Claims are not endorsements.** Record what was claimed, by whom, and with what evidence.
- **Uncertainty remains visible.** Preserve disputes, corrections, missing information, and changing validation status.
- **Failures are evidence.** Documented attempts are part of the corpus when their setup and outcome are adequately preserved.
- **Machine readability without premature rigidity.** Begin with Markdown research notes; derive a versioned schema from a diverse initial corpus.
- **Respect rights and privacy.** Mirror prompts only with a defensible permission or license basis. Otherwise preserve metadata and links.

## Initial release target

Research broadly, vet approximately ten diverse cases, derive a provisional schema, and use those records to prototype a private static site. The site should make summary statistics and a timeline of attempts, announced results, and validation milestones immediately visible.
